AI Revenue Recovery Agent

Autonomous, Intelligent & Compliant Recovery for Razorpay Merchants

Plugging the leaky checkout funnel using agentic AI reasoning, real Razorpay transactional APIs, and strict safety guardrails.

Presented by: Kashish Jindal
The Problem

The Leaky Checkout Funnel

15%

Lost Merchant Sales

Merchants lose 10% to 15% of revenue to failed checkouts: UPI bank network downtime, card limit declines, and cart drop-offs.

Static & "Dumb" Tools

Existing recovery tools blast identical, static email templates to everyone—ignoring root cause, bank health, and context.

Spam & Compliance Risk

Unregulated retries annoy customers, violate communication compliance, and damage the merchant's brand reputation.

Our Solution

Autonomous AI Revenue Recovery

AI Root-Cause Diagnosis

Evaluates exact Razorpay error codes (UPI timeout vs. limit decline) and determines optimal channel & cooldown wait times.

Real Razorpay Integration

Calls official Razorpay Orders API to dynamically generate secure checkout sessions matching the transaction amount.

Adaptive Hinglish Messaging

Generates high-converting, personalized messages tailored for Indian phone numbers to win back distracted buyers.

Strict Compliance Guardrails

Terminates after 3 attempts and stops instantly if a customer replies 'STOP' to guarantee zero spam.

Workflow

The 4-Step Recovery Loop

STEP 1

Payment Fails

Razorpay sends webhook event (or triggered via simulator). Logged under Revenue at Risk.

STEP 2

AI Diagnoses

Agent evaluates error codes, cooldown timer, channel, and drafts Hinglish recovery copy.

STEP 3

Checkout Created

Official Razorpay Order ID & Checkout popup generated dynamically with order metadata.

STEP 4

Payment Saved

Customer pays -> Razorpay redirect marks order recovered live on dashboard stats.

Recovering Margin. Respecting Customers.
